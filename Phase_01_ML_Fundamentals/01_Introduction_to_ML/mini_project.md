# Mini Project: Design Your First ML System

## Problem Statement
You are hired as a Junior ML Product Manager at a startup. You don't need to write production code yet, but you need to design the ML Architecture for a new feature.

**The Product:** An app that predicts whether a flight will be delayed based on historical data.

## Your Task

Write a 1-page design document answering the following:

1. **Problem Type**: Is this Supervised, Unsupervised, or Reinforcement Learning? Is it Classification or Regression?
2. **Features ($X$)**: List 5 specific data points (features) you would need to collect to train your model. (e.g., Weather at departure).
3. **Target ($y$)**: What exactly is the label you are predicting?
4. **Data Collection**: Where and how will you get historical data to train the algorithm?
5. **Evaluation Metric**: How will you prove to the CEO that your model is actually working? (e.g., Accuracy).

## Expected Output
A structured markdown file (`flight_delay_design.md`) outlining your strategic thought process. This trains your "Product Sense," which is critical for Senior AI Engineers.
